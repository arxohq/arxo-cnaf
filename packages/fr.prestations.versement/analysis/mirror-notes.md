# Mirror notes — fund-stated inputs of `fr.prestations.versement` (Q4)

Every head below arrives as case input because the text that would let
this layer derive it is mirror-only or unpinned. Nothing here is a
placeholder for a rule this package will ever own: even after pinning,
(item 4) the debts stay fund-stated — only the labels change.

1. AAH own debt — `indu_aah_retenu`, `indu_aah_frauduleux`.
   Permission L. 821-5-1 MIRROR-ONLY (VIGUEUR 2024-01-01). The scale
   MATH is pinned-grounded (L. 553-2 al. 3 scope sentence covers
   L. 821-5-1 retenues); the recovery permission is not. At pinning:
   relabel, keep fund-stated.
2. Housing own debt — `indu_logement_retenu`,
   `indu_logement_frauduleux`. Permission L. 823-9 CCH MIRROR-ONLY
   ("L. 553-2 applicable au recouvrement"). NO 10 % indemnity even
   in principle (renvoi only). Housing LIMITATION is SILENCE
   (B-PV-LOGEMENT-PRESCRIPTION), not mirror: nothing to pin.
3. Titre II/IV inference (Q3). The L. 553-2 al. 1 overflow list cites
   "book VIII titles II and IV", read as AAH + PA on the mirror
   hierarchy — MIRROR-pending, never pinned. Consequence:
   `pf_indu_reporte_sur_aah` AND `rsa_indu_reporte_sur_aah` stay
   fund-stated with the inference flagged (CH5-1: both legs depend
   on it — the file must not split them); if pinning refutes the
   reading, both carry inputs are withdrawn, not relabelled. (No
   PA-carry head exists in this package at all — item 5, BORDER.)
4. Debts stay fund-stated after pinning (D4). Pinning L. 821-5-1 or
   L. 823-9 would ground the PERMISSION, never the AMOUNT: the fund
   alone knows what it charged to the echeance (subsidiarity,
   B-PV-SUBSIDIARITE). Labels carry "la dette reste fund-stated
   même après épinglage".
5. PA border. L. 845-3/L. 845-4 mirror texts are quoted in the S3
   report only; no heads, no rules (B-PV-PA, CNAF-PPA owns).
6. DSS scale (not mirror: unpinned decree). All `taux_retenue_*` and
   `taux_majoration_*` inputs; percentages and exact base sit in
   decree/DSS bytes (B-PV-DSS-SCALE). Non-PF uplifts arrive
   already-effective (repeat status neighbour-owned).
7. Pre-2024 paragraphs (B-PV-PRE2024). RSA L. 262-46 PRE2024 is
   PINNED but not encoded (module starts 2024-01-01 with L. 553-2);
   reserved, not mirror.
