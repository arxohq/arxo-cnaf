# `fr.css.aah` — disabled-adults allowance (allocation aux adultes handicapés)

Executable formalization of the AAH core: the monthly euro amount
(L. 821-3-1, pinned and built by the chain, B-AAH-MONTANT-EURO resolved), the 80%+ and
50–79%-with-RSDAE tracks (CDAPH decision as established fact/channel,
B-AAH-TETE), annual vs quarterly resource tracks (R. 821-4 / R. 821-4-1),
the spouse-income uncoupling of 01.10.2023 as a double calculation with an
irreversible switch (decrees 2022-1694 / 2023-360, D. 821-2 / D. 821-5
edits), the ESAT cumulation caps (D. 821-5, additive-points reading:
100/115/130/145%), the fund payment duty (D1), the CDAPH attribution
power (P1) and the recipient duties (D2/D3).

A decree names the amount, so the window rule carries it
(`montant_aah_applicable`, consumed by `03-montant.law`) and declares it
published (`aah_montant_publie`); the chain is built independently
(`aah_montant_construit`) and compared (`aah_montant_conforme_publie`); the
generator refuses to emit a number that differs from the decree. A
case-supplied `montant_mensuel_retenu` still prevails (`aah_montant_saisi`) and
is itself compared with the built amount (`aah_saisi_conforme_construit`).
The window 01.07.2022 - 31.03.2023 has no decree: its amount is the 2022-700
figure times 1.04 (article 9 of act 2022-1158).

**Reading (SPEC 152, D-82).** No text states how the product is rounded;
`AahRevalorisationArrondiCentime` rounds each step to the centime, half up -
fund (CNAF) practice. Selected in the context it enables the built chain and
the 2022-07 window; unselected, the decree amounts still apply and the 2022-07
window has no amount. The DSS instructions print the coefficient for the BMAF;
extending it to the AAH is a reading of L. 821-3-1 ("le coefficient mentionné
à l'article L. 161-25"), borne out by every built amount equalling its decree
to the centime. The placeholder `facteur_revalorisation_retenu` and the rule `MontantRevalorise`
(a what-if over a retained amount) were REMOVED on 08.10.2026 (owner decision
D-83): the amount is built by the chain, nothing else revalues it.

**Reading (SPEC 152, D-82/D-83) for the D. 821-2 divisions.** D. 821-2 al. 3
gives the allowance as the "douzième" (annual track, R. 821-4) or the "tiers"
(quarterly track, R. 821-4-1) of the difference between the ceiling and the
resources, and no text states a rounding. By default the division is exact: a
non-terminating quotient is `INEXACT_DIVISION` (SPEC 50/18). The reading
`DouziemeTiersArrondiCentime` (outside any group, `03-montant.law`, fund
practice) rounds the quotient to the centime, half up, in all six rules (current
and former, annual and quarterly, single and couple) and also rounds exact
quotients longer than two places, as the funds do; the cap at the monthly amount
comes after. Unselected, behaviour is unchanged. Scenarios
`tests/aah-division-*.lawtest` (family `fr.css.aah#montant-division`): replica on
fractions, HALF_UP, from the pinned figures (decree 2024-397: 1 016,05).
